# What score do I actually need? — Tamil Nadu cutoffs

**Short answer: ~62/80 in prelims to survive, ~68/80 to be safe. Then ~65/100 in mains to get the
job.** The reasoning, the history, and the arithmetic are below.

> ⚠️ **Source quality — read this first.** IBPS publishes cutoffs inside its result documents, but
> those aren't reachable now. Everything here is compiled from **three coaching aggregators**
> ([CareerPower](https://www.careerpower.in/ibps-rrb-clerk-cut-off.html),
> [Oliveboard](https://www.oliveboard.in/ibps-rrb-clerk-cut-off/),
> [PW](https://www.pw.live/banking/exams/ibps-rrb-clerk-cut-off)) and cross-checked against each
> other. Where they disagree I say so. This is **Level 2 evidence, not official.** Treat these as
> planning targets, not guarantees, and re-check against the official result PDF when it appears.

## 1. Tamil Nadu prelims cutoff — out of 80

General category, Office Assistant, all cycles I could verify:

| Cycle | TN General cutoff / 80 |
|---|---|
| 2018 | 61.75 |
| 2019 | 68.00 |
| 2020 | ~71.25 *(reported as an all-category figure)* |
| 2021 | 70.50 |
| 2022 | 61.25 |
| 2023 | 61.00 – 62.25 *(sources differ)* |
| 2024 | 61.00 |
| **2025 cycle** *(prelims Dec 2025)* | **62.25** |

**Pattern:** four of the last five cycles have landed at **61–62.25**. The 68–71 years (2019–2021)
were harder-paper or fewer-vacancy years. **Plan for ~62, and don't be shocked by 68.**

### The uncomfortable context

Tamil Nadu is consistently **among the highest-cutoff states in India** for this exam — the national
range runs roughly 58–77, and TN sits at the top end. A score that comfortably clears in Bihar or
Madhya Pradesh will not clear here. Do not calibrate against national averages or against YouTube
videos made for a national audience.

### On the OBC prelims number

The aggregators are **unreliable here** and I won't pretend otherwise. CareerPower shows TN 2023 OBC
at 70.50 while PW shows 63.25 for the same year; several rows show OBC *above* General, which is
implausible for a cutoff and suggests those cells are actually maximum scores or simply mislabelled.

**So plan against the General number.** Three reasons this is the right call:
1. It's the figure all three sources agree on.
2. It's conservative — if the OBC cutoff is genuinely lower, you clear with room to spare.
3. If you beat the UR cutoff you're counted against UR and don't consume a reserved seat at all.

## 2. Tamil Nadu mains cutoff — out of 100 (normalized)

**Important:** the mains paper is **200 marks**, but IBPS publishes cutoffs **out of 100**, after
equi-percentile normalization across shifts. All three sources confirm this. So a "62" below is
roughly — *but not exactly* — 124/200 raw. Normalization is not a linear halving, so treat the
conversion as a guide, never as arithmetic.

These are the **minimum scores among provisionally allotted candidates** — i.e. the effective cutoff:

| Cycle | UR / 100 | **OBC / 100** | EWS | SC | ST |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | 61.78 | **60.69** | — | 53.53 | 42.28 |
| 2020 | 66.38 | **64.78** | 52.75 | 52.35 | 48.16 |
| 2021 | 66.10 | **65.03** | 54.82 | 54.88 | 46.91 |
| 2023 | 60.41 | **59.69** | 48.41 | 50.88 | 50.47 |
| 2024 | 66.48 | **60.13** | 58.25 | 56.94 | 48.63 |
| **2025 cycle** | 63.82 | **62.57** | 43.47 | 55.25 | 49.50 |

**OBC range: 59.69 – 65.03. Median ≈ 62.**

### ⚠️ The finding that matters most to you

**In Tamil Nadu the OBC cushion is tiny — often 1 to 3 marks below UR.**

| Cycle | UR | OBC | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | 61.78 | 60.69 | **1.09** |
| 2020 | 66.38 | 64.78 | **1.60** |
| 2021 | 66.10 | 65.03 | **1.07** |
| 2023 | 60.41 | 59.69 | **0.72** |
| 2025 | 63.82 | 62.57 | **1.25** |

Only 2024 showed a real gap (6.35). In every other year the OBC advantage was **about one mark.**

Tamil Nadu's OBC pool is enormous and fiercely competitive — the reservation does not buy you the
cushion candidates in other states get. **Prepare as though you are a General candidate.** Treat any
OBC benefit as a bonus you find at the end, not a margin you plan around. This is the single most
important thing on this page.

## 3. Your targets

### Prelims — 6 / 12 / 13 December 2026

| Level | Score / 80 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Danger | below 60 | You will not clear in Tamil Nadu |
| 🟡 Minimum | **62** | Clears the recent median. No margin for a hard paper. |
| 🟢 **Target** | **68** | Clears every TN cutoff of the last 8 years except 2020 |
| 🔵 Safe | 72+ | Clears anything |

**Aim for 68.** Prelims is qualifying only — marks above the cutoff earn you nothing — but the cost
of a 4-mark miss is the entire year, and papers vary. Buy the insurance.

### The arithmetic of a 68

With 0.25 negative marking: `score = correct − 0.25 × wrong`

| Attempt | Accuracy | Correct | Wrong | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 80 | 85% | 68 | 12 | 65.0 |
| 74 | 95% | 70 | 4 | **69.0** ✅ |
| 72 | 96% | 69 | 3 | **68.3** ✅ |
| 70 | 91% | 64 | 6 | 62.5 🟡 |
| 66 | 94% | 62 | 4 | 61.0 🔴 |
| 60 | 100% | 60 | 0 | 60.0 🔴 |

Read the last row carefully. **Sixty questions perfectly right is not enough in Tamil Nadu.** You
cannot play safe and low-volume here. The winning zone is **70–74 attempts at 93–96% accuracy** —
high volume *and* high accuracy, in 45 minutes.

### Section split for a 68

| Section | Attempt | Correct | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reasoning** (40) | 38 | 36 | 35.5 |
| **Numerical** (40) | 34 | 32 | 31.5 |
| **Total** | 72 | 68 | **67.0** |

Reasoning is the faster-scoring half — puzzles are slow but syllogism, inequality, coding-decoding
and series are quick, high-accuracy marks. Numerical carries your weak history (10th: 45/100,
11th: 35/100), so it gets the lower attempt target **and** the biggest share of your prep hours.
That is not a contradiction: you train hardest where you're weakest, and attempt most where you're
strongest.

### Mains — 30 January 2027

| Level | Score / 100 normalized | ≈ raw / 200 |
|---|---|---|
| 🟡 Minimum | 62 | ~124 |
| 🟢 **Target** | **68** | ~136 |
| 🔵 Safe | 72+ | ~144 |

**Aim for 68/100.** That clears every TN OBC cutoff on record (max 65.03) with a real margin.

Plus the hard constraint from the notification: *"Each candidate will be required to obtain a
**minimum score in each test**."* IBPS doesn't publish those sectional minimums, and they are
usually low enough not to bind — but they mean **you cannot zero any section.** Your lowest section
is your real risk, not your total.

## 4. What this means for the plan

Three things follow from these numbers, and they're already reflected in
[STUDY-PLAN.md](STUDY-PLAN.md):

1. **Speed is the binding constraint, not knowledge.** You need ~72 attempts in 45 minutes — under
   38 seconds per question, sustained, including the slow puzzle sets. No amount of conceptual
   understanding delivers that. Only drilling does. This is why the 20-minute daily tables/squares/
   fractions drill is non-negotiable and why Stage 3 is nothing but timed mocks.
2. **Accuracy above 93% is not optional.** At 85% accuracy you'd need to attempt all 80 and still
   fall short. Every mock analysis must count CARELESS errors separately — in this exam they are the
   cheapest marks available and the most commonly squandered.
3. **Don't plan around your OBC status.** A ~1-mark cushion is noise. Prepare to a General-category
   standard and let the category help at the margin.

### Track it

Add this to your weekly tracker in [STUDY-PLAN.md §4](STUDY-PLAN.md):

```
Mock score:   ___/80     Attempted: ___   Accuracy: ___%
Gap to 62 (minimum): ___     Gap to 68 (target): ___
```

Your baseline mock will likely land in the 30s or 40s. The distance from there to 68 is 14 weeks of
work, which is exactly what the plan is for.
