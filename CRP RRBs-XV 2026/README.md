## 2. Am I eligible?

### Educational qualification (official, Section VI, p.16)

> **Office Assistants (Multipurpose)** — "Bachelor's degree in **any discipline** from a recognized
> University or its equivalent"
> "(a) Proficiency in local language as prescribed by the participating RRB/s
> (b) **Desirable:** Working knowledge of Computer."
> Post-qualification experience: **----** (none required)

| Check | Verdict |
|---|---|
| BCA (Data Science) | 🟢 **ELIGIBLE** — "any discipline" is satisfied by the BCA alone |
| M.Sc. Applied Data Science | Not needed for eligibility; a bonus, not a requirement |
| Experience | 🟢 None required |
| Computer knowledge | "Desirable" only — but see §5, it is where you win |

Qualification is assessed **as on the last date of registration, 21.09.2026.** Your BCA is long
complete, so there is no issue.

### Local language — Tamil (official, p.18)

> "Candidates who have already studied the language of the State in **standard VIII or any level
> above standard VIII** in Government recognized Boards of Education/School... would be considered
> proficient in that local language."

If you studied Tamil up to Class 8 or beyond, you are proficient. Keep the marksheet/TC that proves
it — it is checked at document verification, **not** at application time.

### Age (official, Section II — as on 01.09.2026)

> Office Assistants (Multipurpose): **between 18 and 28 years**.
> "Candidates should not have been born earlier than **02.09.1998** and later than **01.09.2008**
> (both dates inclusive)"

Relaxation: SC/ST +5 yrs · OBC (non-creamy layer) +3 yrs · PwBD +10 yrs · ESM/DESM = actual service
period + 3 yrs (8 yrs for DESM SC/ST), max age 50.

⚠️ **VERIFY THIS YOURSELF BEFORE PAYING.** If you were born before 02.09.1998 you are out for
Office Assistant in the General category. This is the only clause that can disqualify you.

### Fee

Rs. **850/-** (all others) · Rs. **175/-** (SC/ST/PwBD/ESM/DESM) — inclusive of GST.

## 5. Exam structure (official, Section D)

### Preliminary — 80 Q / 80 marks / **45 minutes** (composite timing)

| # | Test | Q | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reasoning | 40 | 40 | 25 min |
| 2 | Numerical Ability | 40 | 40 | 20 min |
| | **Total** | **80** | **80** | **45 min** |

Medium (Tamil Nadu, official list of test versions, row 23): **English, Hindi and Tamil.**
Negative marking: **0.25** per wrong answer. Blank = no penalty.

### Main — 200 Q / 200 marks / **120 minutes**

| # | Test | Q | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reasoning | 40 | **50** | 30 min |
| 2 | **Computer Knowledge** | 40 | **20** | 15 min |
| 3 | General Awareness | 40 | **40** | 15 min |
| 4 | English **or** Hindi Language (pick one) | 40 | **40** | 30 min |
| 5 | Numerical Ability | 40 | **50** | 30 min |
| | **Total** | **200** | **200** | **120 min** |

### 🔑 The two rules that decide your whole strategy

Official, Section G:
> "For the post of Office Assistants (Multipurpose) — **Marks obtained only in the online main
> examination will be considered for final merit listing.**"

Official, Section F:
> "Each candidate will be required to obtain a **minimum score in each test** of Online Main
> examination to be considered to be shortlisted for provisional allotment."

**Therefore:**

1. **Prelims is a gate, not a score.** Clear the cutoff and forget it. Every prelims mark above the
   cutoff is worth exactly zero toward your final rank.
2. **Mains is the entire exam.** And you must clear a **sectional** minimum in all five — you cannot
   use a strong Computer score to rescue a failed English section.
3. **There is no interview.** 200 marks on one January morning is the complete selection process.

### Your unfair advantage

Computer Knowledge is 40 questions. For a BCA (DBMS, OS, Networks, Data Structures, C/Java/Python)
plus M.Sc. Applied Data Science candidate, that section is close to free marks that Arts and
Commerce graduates grind at for months. Budget roughly **15 hours total** on it, not 100. Move the
saved time into Numerical Ability and General Awareness — that is where you will actually bleed.

**Do both.** If Scale-I lands, it is a strictly better job — officer cadre, faster promotion, higher
pay. If it doesn't, the November attempt makes you sharper for December.

## 9. 14-week plan (02 Sep 2026 → 06 Dec 2026 prelims)

Prelims is a **speed test**, not a knowledge test. 80 questions in 45 minutes is **33 seconds per
question.** There is nothing in the prelims syllabus a BCA graduate cannot understand — the entire
difficulty is doing it fast enough. Plan accordingly.

### Phase A — Weeks 1–2 (Sep 2 – Sep 15): apply, then measure

- [ ] Verify your DOB against the 02.09.1998 cutoff **before paying anything**
- [ ] **Apply for Office Assistant** at ibps.in (deadline 21 Sep) ← do this first
- [ ] **Apply for Officer Scale-I** in the same window
- [ ] Locate and scan your Class 8+ Tamil proof
- [ ] Sit `pyq/prelims/2025-Prelims-OA-06Dec-Shift1-English.pdf` **cold, timed, 45 minutes.**
      Score it honestly. Write the number down. That is your baseline.

### Phase B — Weeks 3–8 (Sep 16 – Oct 25): build the machine

Daily, ~2 hrs:

- **50 min Numerical Ability** — Simplification/Approximation, Number Series, Quadratic Comparison,
  Data Interpretation (bar / line / pie / table), Arithmetic (percentage, ratio, average,
  profit-loss, SI/CI, time-and-work, time-speed-distance, mixtures, partnership)
- **50 min Reasoning** — Puzzles and Seating Arrangement (this alone is 20–25 of the 40 marks;
  nothing else comes close), Syllogism, Inequality, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations,
  Order and Ranking, Alphanumeric Series, Direction Sense
- **20 min** — a Tamil-medium video **only on the specific topic that beat you that day**

Non-negotiable habit: **20 minutes daily of tables to 25, squares to 40, cubes to 25, and
fractions ↔ percentages from 1/2 to 1/25.** This is the single highest-return activity in the whole
plan. Not knowing 1/16 = 6.25% instantly costs you real seconds on real questions.

### Phase C — Weeks 9–14 (Oct 26 – Dec 5): mocks and the Scale-I rehearsal

- **3 full timed prelims mocks per week** — always 45 minutes, always in one sitting
- **Analysis takes longer than the mock itself.** For every wrong answer, label it: *careless /
  didn't know / too slow.* If "too slow" dominates, you are attempting too many questions — cut
  attempts and raise accuracy.
- **21–22 Nov: Officer Scale-I prelims** — a free live rehearsal under real pressure
- Work backwards through `pyq/prelims/` from 2025 to 2016
- Last 5 days: no new topics. Revision, formula sheet, and sleep schedule only.

### Phase D — Weeks 15–21 (Dec 14 → Jan 30, only if prelims clears)

This is the phase that actually decides the job: 7 weeks, five sections, sectional cutoffs.

- **General Awareness (40 Q)** — the make-or-break section. Start banking awareness plus the last
  six months' current affairs **now**, 15 min/day. Do not leave it to December. Adda247 Tamil's
  daily current affairs is the efficient route.
- **Computer Knowledge (40 Q)** — ~15 hours total, at most. You already know the material; skim a
  bank-exam computer capsule for *their* terminology (they ask MS Office shortcuts and networking
  generalities, not your ML pipeline).
- **English (40 Q)** — Reading Comprehension, Error Spotting, Cloze, Para Jumbles, Fillers
- **Reasoning + Numerical** — carried over from prelims prep, now with harder DI and puzzles
- Work through `pyq/mains/` from 2023 back to 2018
