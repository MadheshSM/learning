## ⚠️ Read this once

**IBPS has never released an official question paper.** Not for RRB, not for PO, not ever. There is
no such thing as the "actual" IBPS paper in public circulation, and any site claiming to sell you
one is lying.

Every paper here is **memory-based** — reconstructed after each shift by coaching institutes from
candidate recall, then cross-checked across many candidates. This is what every serious aspirant in
India actually practises on, and it is genuinely good material: the topic mix, difficulty and
question style track the real exam closely, because they *came from* the real exam.

Two limits to keep in mind:
- **Answer keys can be wrong.** They're institute-produced. If a solution looks incorrect, it may be.
- **A few questions per paper are garbled or missing** where recall was poor. Don't panic if a
  question seems unanswerable — sometimes it is.

Score yourself out of what's actually there, not a notional 80.

## How to run a mock properly

1. **45 minutes. One sitting. Phone in another room.** A paused mock teaches you nothing about a
   45-minute exam.
2. Mark answers on a separate sheet, not on the PDF — you'll want to reuse the paper.
3. Score it: `correct − 0.25 × wrong`. Blanks cost nothing.
4. **Analyse for 60–90 minutes** — longer than the mock. Tag every wrong and skipped question:
   **CARELESS / DIDN'T KNOW / TOO SLOW / BAD PICK.**
5. Log it in [INDEX.csv](INDEX.csv)'s last column and in your weekly tracker.

Your target is **68/80**. See [CUTOFFS.md](../CUTOFFS.md) for why, and for the attempt/accuracy
arithmetic that gets you there.
