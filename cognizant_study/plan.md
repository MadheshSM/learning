# Learning Plan — Cognizant AI Roles (2-Year Study Guide)

**Purpose:** Build and retain interview-ready knowledge across the AI/Data Science role family at
Cognizant, over a self-paced 2-year horizon.
**Format:** 13 phases (one per topic in `study_guide.md`), each tagged Year 1 or Year 2. Unlike the
Node/Angular/Python tracks, this isn't "read once and move on" — it's a body of knowledge you need to
still have cold in an interview a year from now, so **review cadence matters as much as first pass.**

> Source: `study_guide.md` in this folder (a plain-text mirror of `cognizant_ai_study_guide.docx`) is
> the actual question/answer content. This plan only adds pacing, a "Done" bar, and a review cadence
> around it — read the guide itself for the material.

---

## "Done" definition

A concept (question) is **Done** when you can answer it out loud, unaided, in your own words — not
recognize the answer when you read it. For `[CONFIRMED]` questions (came directly from real Cognizant
interview reports), the bar is higher: you should be able to answer it *and* field one natural
follow-up the mentor invents on the spot, since these are the questions most likely to actually come up.

## Review cadence (the part that matters most here)

A 2-year runway means the real risk isn't "never learning it," it's "learned it in month 3, forgot it
by month 14." Build review in from the start:

- **Every session:** before starting new material, the mentor cold-quizzes 2–3 previously-Done
  concepts picked at random (weighted toward `[CONFIRMED]` ones) — this is a session-start ritual, not
  an afterthought.
- **Monthly:** skim the Dashboard sheet for any phase untouched in 60+ days and schedule a review pass,
  even a short one.
- **Months 18–24:** per the source guide, intensify Phase 12 (Behavioral/HR) specifically — these
  answers get stale and generic if not rehearsed close to when you're actually interviewing.
- **`[CONFIRMED]` questions (15 total, listed in the Self-Assessment Checklist):** these get reviewed
  more often than the rest, since they map to what real candidates were actually asked.

## How to use this plan

1. Open a session in this folder with Claude Code — `CLAUDE.md` picks up automatically and runs the
   session-start ritual (checks the tracker, cold-quizzes old material, proposes what's next).
2. Per concept: the mentor explains it briefly beyond just reading the Q&A back to you, asks you to
   explain it unaided, pushes on a natural follow-up if it's `[CONFIRMED]`, then marks the tracker row.
3. Track progress in `learning_tracker.xlsx` — the Dashboard sheet shows % complete per phase, and the
   dashboard at `../dashboard/cognizant.html` visualizes it alongside your other tracks.
4. Log study time in the tracker's **Time Log** sheet (Date, Hours Spent, Notes) so the dashboard can
   estimate pace and a projected "ready by" date.

There's no fixed daily pace prescribed here — this is explicitly a 2-year runway, not an 8-week
sprint. What matters is that review actually happens, not that you rush the first pass.
